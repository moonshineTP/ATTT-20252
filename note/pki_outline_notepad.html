Chương III Trust Architecture & X.509 PKI ~1800 từ · 6 trang · Section 2 - CORE
3.1
Digital Certificate & X.509
›Certificate = chữ ký CA lên (identity + public key) - định nghĩa trực quan
›Cấu trúc X.509 v3: subject, issuer, serial, validity, public key, extensions, signature
›Phân biệt self-signed (root CA) vs CA-signed (end-entity)
›Extensions quan trọng: SAN, Key Usage, Basic Constraints
3.2
Certificate Authority & Chain of Trust
›Hierarchy: Root CA - Intermediate CA - End-entity certificate
›Tại sao có Intermediate CA: offline Root, blast radius containment khi compromise
›Path validation algorithm: chain building từ end-entity lên root
›Browser trust store: trust anchor là gì, ai quyết định root nào được tin
3.3
PKI Components đầy đủ
›Registration Authority (RA): identity verification trước khi CA ký
›Certificate Repository: LDAP, public directory
›Revocation: CRL (batch, latency issue) vs OCSP (realtime, privacy issue) vs OCSP Stapling
›Revocation là điểm yếu thực tế - soft-fail behavior của browsers
3.4
Trust Models so sánh
›Hierarchical (Web PKI): transitive trust rõ ràng, single root of trust per CA
›Web of Trust (PGP): decentralized, peer endorsement, bootstrapping problem
›Bridge CA: cross-domain enterprise, cross-certify giữa các hierarchy
›Trade-off: centralized trust vs decentralized trust - khi nào dùng gì
3.5
Evolution of Authentication Solutions
›Layer 1 - Direct/Host-based trust: known_hosts, O(n²) problem
›Layer 2 - Centralized KDC (Kerberos): giải quyết scale, tạo SPOF
›Layer 3 - Web of Trust (PGP): distributed nhưng trust không transitive đáng tin
›Layer 4 - Certificate PKI: kết hợp scalability + verifiability, justify thiết kế ở 3.1-3.4
